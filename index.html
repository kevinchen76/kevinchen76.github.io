About Me

Hi there! I’m Kevin Chen, a data scientist and AI ethics researcher based in Austin, Texas. My mission is to build trustworthy, reproducible, and practical data systems that help organizations make better decisions across AI safety, analytics, governance, and operations.

As a Research Professional at the Urban Information Lab at the University of Texas at Austin, I work at the intersection of data science, large language model evaluation, and responsible AI. I have co-authored 13+ peer-reviewed publications on LLM safety and AI ethics, accumulating 95+ citations within 12 months. My work includes building evaluation frameworks, designing text-mining pipelines, validating model outputs, and creating visualizations that communicate complex findings to both technical and non-technical audiences.

I work across the full data workflow: ingestion, cleaning, schema validation, analysis, visualization, and reporting. My toolkit spans Python, R, STATA, pandas, scikit-learn, and Excel for data wrangling, statistical analysis, and modeling; SQL, MySQL, Snowflake, and Databricks for databases, querying, and scalable data workflows; Tableau, Power BI, and matplotlib for dashboards and data visualization; AWS and Azure for cloud platforms; Git/GitHub for version control and reproducible development; and Java, JavaScript, and HTML/CSS for software and web development.

I recently graduated from the University of Texas at Austin with a B.S. in Economics and minors in Computer Science, Data Science, and Applied Statistics. My background combines quantitative analysis, software development, policy research, and AI safety, allowing me to translate messy data and emerging technology questions into clear, actionable insights.

I’m especially interested in roles across data analytics, data science, data engineering, DevOps, financial analysis, and procurement/sustainment analytics. If you’re interested in discussing AI ethics, LLM safety, data systems, or analytics-driven decision-making, feel free to reach out!
