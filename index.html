About Me

I am a data scientist and researcher passionate about AI ethics, safety, and the responsible development of large language models. My work bridges quantitative analysis and policy to ensure emerging technologies serve humanity equitably.

As a Research Professional at the Urban Information Lab at UT Austin, I have co-authored 13+ peer-reviewed publications on LLM safety and AI ethics, accumulating 77+ citations within 12 months. I build data pipelines, design evaluation frameworks, and create visualizations that communicate research findings to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
